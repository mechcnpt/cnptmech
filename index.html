About Us

The Department of Mechanical Engineering at P.T. Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker Polytechnic College in Chennai is a cornerstone of the institution's technical education offerings. It provides a diploma program that emphasizes both theoretical knowledge and practical skills, preparing students for careers in industries like manufacturing, automotive, and aerospace.

The department is equipped with modern laboratories, workshops, and tools to ensure hands-on learning. Students are trained in areas such as machine design, thermodynamics, and manufacturing processes. The faculty members are experienced and dedicated to fostering a strong foundation in engineering principles.

The college also focuses on extracurricular activities, including technical clubs and industry visits, to enhance students' exposure to real-world applications. Placement support is provided to help graduates secure jobs in reputed companies.

Er. S.Saravanan

M.E., M.B.A.,

Dear Students, Faculty Members, and Esteemed Visitors,

It gives me immense pride and joy to welcome you to the Department of Mechanical Engineering at P.T. Lee Chengalvaraya Naicker Polytechnic College. As the Head of this vibrant department, I firmly believe that mechanical engineering is not just a branch of study—it is the backbone of innovation that drives our modern world. From the smallest precision components to massive industrial machinery, mechanical engineers shape the future of manufacturing, energy, transportation, and beyond.

Our department is committed to nurturing young minds with a perfect blend of theoretical excellence and hands-on practical expertise. We have state-of-the-art laboratories, well-equipped workshops, and a dedicated team of faculty members who constantly strive to bring out the best in every student. Our curriculum is regularly updated to meet industry standards, and we emphasize project-based learning, industrial visits, internships, and value-added courses to ensure that our graduates are not just degree holders, but industry-ready professionals.

I encourage each student to make the most of every opportunity—participate in technical clubs, engage in research activities, and develop problem-solving skills. Remember, discipline, integrity, and continuous learning are the pillars of a successful engineering career. Our placement cell works tirelessly to connect talented students with top recruiters, and I am proud to see many of our alumni excelling in reputed organizations across the globe.

As we move forward, our vision is to foster innovation, promote sustainable engineering practices, and create leaders who will contribute to society's progress. I invite you to explore our website, connect with us, and be a part of this exciting journey. Together, let us build a future powered by mechanical excellence.

Wishing you all the very best in your academic and professional endeavors.

— Er.S.Saravanan
Head of the Department, Mechanical Engineering

Institution Vision & Mission

Vision Mission
  • To achieve our goal of becoming a model polytechnic institution, we focus on offering both syllabus-oriented classes and job-oriented courses to equip students with the skills needed to excel in engineering fields and adapt to the latest technological developments.
  • Providing excellence in Engineering education, Fostering ethical values and a strong sense of responsibility among students and staff.
  • Building a team of qualified, passionate faculty dedicated to delivering both curriculum based education and career oriented training.
  • Developing and maintaining cutting edge infrastructure that supports innovation and hands on learning.

Department Vision & Mission

Vision Mission
  • To provide quality education to the students of Mechanical Engineering so as to make them employable and to enhance their technical skills.
  • To cultivate dynamic learning environment that empowers students with fundamental and advanced mechanical Engineering knowledge.
  • To foster creativity and equip them with hands on skills to meet real world challenges while upholding ethical standards and sustainable principles.
  • To Support and promote excellence in teaching, research and innovation encouraging collaboration among faculty members and students.
  • To develop and maintain the state of the art facilities, Laboratories and technological resources that enhance research and education.
